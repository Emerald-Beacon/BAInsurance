## Findings

### 1. `/thank-you/` is indexable, in the sitemap, and fires a conversion event — Critical

`dist/thank-you/index.html` carries no `robots` directive and appears in `sitemap-0.xml`. It also
fires the Meta Pixel `Lead` event on load, and as of this branch that event now carries
`content_category`.

**Why this is the most urgent item:** anyone who reaches `/thank-you/` from a search result, a
shared link, or a browser back-button fires a `Lead`. Your conversion count inflates with
non-conversions, and the per-line attribution the quote flow was built to produce is corrupted at
the source. This got worse the moment lead tracking started mattering.

**Fix:** add a `noindex` prop to `Layout.astro`, set it on `/thank-you/`, and exclude the route
from the sitemap via the existing `@astrojs/sitemap` `filter` option.

### 2. Titles are truncated in search results on 120 of 139 pages — Important

| Page type | n | Title > 60 chars | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blog posts | 119 | **111** | 82 |
| Quote pages | 9 | **8** | 65 |
| Service/company | 8 | 1 | 44 |

**Root cause** is one line — `src/layouts/Layout.astro:33`:

```ts
const fullTitle = title.includes('B&A') ? title : `${title} | B&A Insurance Producers`;
```

The suffix adds 26 characters unconditionally. Post titles averaging 56 characters land at 82,
and Google truncates around 60. The brand name is the part that gets cut, so the suffix is
costing characters without delivering the branding it exists for.

**Fix:** append the suffix only when the result still fits, and shorten it:

```ts
const SUFFIX = ' | B&A Insurance';
const fullTitle = title.includes('B&A') ? title
  : (title.length + SUFFIX.length <= 60 ? title + SUFFIX : title);
```

This recovers full titles on every page without touching 119 post records. Posts whose own titles
exceed 60 characters still need editorial shortening; that is a content task, not a code one.

### 3. Meta descriptions are truncated on 128 of 139 pages — Important

Blog posts: **119 of 119** over 160 characters, median 199. Service pages: **8 of 8**, median 185.

Blog posts pass `excerpt` straight through as the description, and excerpts were written as
excerpts, not as SERP copy. The tail is cut, which usually means the call to action is cut.

**Fix:** truncate at a word boundary near 155 characters for post descriptions, and hand-rewrite
the 8 service/company descriptions. The new quote landing pages are already compliant
(119–143 chars) because the data module enforces the range in a test — `/quote/` itself is the
exception at 199 and should be brought in line.

### 4. Every blog post declares `og:type: website` — Important

All 119 posts emit `<meta property="og:type" content="website">`. They are articles. There is also
no `article:published_time`, despite `datePublished` being correct in the Article JSON-LD.

This weakens how posts render when shared and how they are classified as timestamped content —
which matters for the freshness plays `rankless/03-gaps.md` is built around.

**Fix:** add `ogType` and `publishedTime` props to `Layout.astro`; pass `'article'` and `post.date`
from `src/pages/[slug].astro`.

### 5. `/blog/` renders all 119 posts on one 144 KB page — Minor, deliberate

The largest HTML document on the site by a wide margin. `public/_redirects` shows this was a
considered decision (old WordPress `/blog/page/N/` URLs 301 here, documented as real destination
matches rather than soft 404s), so it is recorded rather than recommended against. Worth
revisiting when the post count grows, ideally with pagination that keeps those redirects valid.

### 6. The content pipeline has been dead for four months — Critical, and not a code problem

Newest post: **2026-05-27**. Today: **2026-09-30**. 119 posts, 26 planned slugs in
`EDITORIAL-CALENDAR-2026-H2.md`, **zero published**.

`rankless/04-strategy.md` opens by refusing to proceed past this:

> Finishing the routine setup would restart publishing without addressing whatever stopped it in
> May. Establish that first; this strategy is worthless against a pipeline that does not run.

Every fix above is worth doing and none of them substitutes for this. The technical baseline is in
good shape; the site is not publishing.

---

## Recommended order

1. **`/thank-you/` noindex + sitemap exclusion** — protects the conversion data the new quote flow
   produces. Do this before driving any traffic to `/quote/`.
2. **Title suffix logic** — one line, fixes 120 pages.
3. **`og:type` and `article:published_time`** — small, and it supports the freshness strategy.
4. **Description truncation** — mechanical for posts, editorial for the 8 service pages.
5. **Find out what stopped publishing in May.** Nothing above matters as much as this.
